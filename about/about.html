About NAF

Practical IT training for beginners, career changers, and community builders.

NAF Tech Academy bridges the gap between ambition and opportunity with hands-on learning, mentorship, and career support.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and practical IT education that opens doors for everyone, especially beginners, immigrants, and career-changers in our city.

What students can expect

  • Small cohorts where questions are welcome.
  • Hands-on labs connected to real IT tasks.
  • Certification-focused guidance without overwhelming jargon.
  • Career coaching that starts before graduation.
